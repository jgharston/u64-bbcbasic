# Ultimate Audio demo: credits

Music (ProTracker MODs, as shipped with idreamtin8bits' Ultimate Demo
2026 and the Land of Ice and Fire demo):

- `4ev.mod`, "Forever Young": sampled and remixed by Slice, August 1995
  (the module's own text: "original: Interactive").
- `wildpersp.mod` (`wild_perspective.mod`), "Wild Perspective": by Greg
  Tuby, 1996, Amiga Music Workshop, for Amiga Format readers.

Sound effects (`*.WAV`): from "The Essential Retro Video Game Sound
Effects Collection [512 sounds]" by Juhani Junkala, CC0 (public domain
dedication), https://opengameart.org/content/512-sound-effects-8-bit-style
-- converted to 8-bit mono 11025 Hz WAV with ffmpeg:

| File | Original |
|---|---|
| LASER.WAV | Weapons/Lasers/sfx_wpn_laser1.wav |
| BOOM.WAV | Explosions/Short/sfx_exp_short_hard3.wav |
| COIN.WAV | General Sounds/Coins/sfx_coin_single2.wav |
| JUMP.WAV | Movement/Jumping and Landing/sfx_movement_jump4.wav |
| BLIP.WAV | General Sounds/Menu Sounds/sfx_menu_move1.wav |
| POWERUP.WAV | General Sounds/Positive Sounds/sfx_sounds_powerup1.wav |
| HIT.WAV | General Sounds/Simple Damage Sounds/sfx_damage_hit1.wav |
| ALARM.WAV | General Sounds/Alarms/Alarms/sfx_alarm_loop1.wav |

The MOD player is ModPlayer_16k by Freshness (6510nl), CSDb release
131522, ported to the MOS (src/mos/modengine.s).
