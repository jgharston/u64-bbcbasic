# 9. Sound

`SOUND` and `ENVELOPE` as on a BBC Micro, on the SIDs. In SID mode (the
start-up mode) the engine uses all voices of up to 8 SIDs (24 voices),
with instruments; `*SOUND BBC` sounds like a BBC Micro. Songs are
written with `SOUND` while recording and play in the background.

## 9.1 Notes and envelopes

@@help SOUND ENVELOPE ADVAL *SPEECH

## 9.2 The sound engine

@@help *SOUND *SID

## 9.3 Instruments and songs

@@help *INSTRUMENT *SONG
